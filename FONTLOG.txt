Basic Font Information
----------------------
PUDDY Odysseus is a geometric sans drawn by THE MASTER CUBE for PUDDY Inc. from the circle, the square
and the triangle, the official typeface of PUDDY. Every outline is computed by PUDDY Inc.'s own construction; no other typeface was traced
or measured. Four weights, Regular, Medium, Bold and Black, and one variable font across them. The
capitals, the lowercase, the figures, basic punctuation and the alchemical sign Caput Mortuum,
🝎 (U+1F74E), are drawn in every weight, and the letters are kerned by rule.

ChangeLog
---------
2026-10-10 (THE MASTER CUBE, PUDDY✵ODYSSEUS) PUDDY Odysseus, version 1.100: four weights and Caput Mortuum
- Four weights, each a static font in OTF, TTF, WOFF and WOFF2: Regular 400, Medium 500, Bold 700 and
  Black 900. The strokes follow the stem by rule: the verticals by their ratio in the Bold, the
  horizontals thinning against the stem as the weight rises, so the counters stay open; the widths
  move with the strokes; the spacing follows the counters. No enclosed counter of any weight holds
  less than a circle of 0.30 of its stem.
- The variable font PUDDYOdysseus[wght].ttf: one axis, wght, 400 to 900, the four weights as its
  named instances, each drawing within one unit of its static font.
- The classic construction: the x-height 450 and the ascender 790, so the ascenders rise well above
  the capitals; the O, C, G and Q true circles; the Q's tail a straight stroke crossing the ring; the j
  a straight stem; the 4 open; the 1's flag cut square.
- U+1F74E: an original drawing of the alchemical sign for caput mortuum, made in the font's geometry.
- Caput Mortuum (🝎, U+1F74E), the alchemical sign, a typographic glyph in every weight, upright as
  the original sign: a ring with three equal round dots inside it, two side by side above the centre
  and one on the axis below them, centred on the cap height, drawn from the same
  three figures as every other glyph at that weight's strokes, from the baseline's overshoot to the
  cap line's. The cmap carries it in a format 12 subtable, since it lies
  outside the Basic Multilingual Plane. 88 glyphs in every weight.

2026-10-02 (THE MASTER CUBE) PUDDY Odysseus Bold, version 1.000
- The first full set, in Bold, 87 glyphs: the capitals, the lowercase, the lining figures and basic
  punctuation, with a kern feature of 125 pairs measured from the outlines by rule. It was not
  published; its design is carried forward into the Bold of 1.100.
- Versions 0.200 to 0.303 (2026-09-30 to 2026-10-02) were development builds and were not published.

Queued: brackets and braces; hinting; the fi and fl ligatures.
